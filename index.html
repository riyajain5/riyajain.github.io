03 / Experience & Projects

Things I've worked on

IIM Bangalore · Valentine's Week

Micro-Venture — ₹250 Budget, 30 Minutes, 100% Profit

Sold chocolate-covered strawberries during Valentine's week at IIM Bangalore — with a strict budget of ₹250 and a 30-minute operating window. Not a case study. Not a simulation. Real money, real pressure.

₹250 Invested
₹500 Revenue
100% Profit
<30 min Execution

The original plan was a Valentine-themed stall at a neighbouring campus with better footfall. On execution morning, the collaborator went unresponsive — taking the location access and rose inventory with him. Instead of scrapping the venture, we redesigned on the spot: built a handheld poster, picked up the strawberry box, and went mobile.

Repositioned the concept from "Valentine's for couples" to "every meaningful bond deserves celebrating" — which opened up pitching to friend groups, girl groups, and mixed crowds, not just couples. Adjusted pricing from ₹150 to ₹100 after reading live hesitation signals. Moved strategically from canteen → utility zone → lawn area, where relaxed group dynamics finally converted.

Biggest takeaway: execution quality matters more than idea strength. Plans collapse — how you respond in that moment is the actual test.
